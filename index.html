Research

Diffusion-based synthetic data augmentation for polyp segmentation

MSc dissertation, University of Glasgow · Sept 2026

Generated realistic colonoscopy images with matching polyp masks to train segmentation models when labelled data is scarce.

  1. Adapted Stable Diffusion 2.1 with LoRA and used ControlNet to place each polyp exactly where its mask says.
  2. With only 100 real images, adding 500 synthetic pairs raised nnU-Net Dice from 0.841 to 0.860.
  3. Showed that image–mask alignment matters more than variety: poorly aligned pairs made results worse.

Finding BERT errors using activation vectors

MS thesis, San Jose State University · May 2023

Best Application Paper Award, IEEE CISOSE 2023
  1. Fine-tuned DNABERT on E. coli DNA data and clustered its activations with t-SNE and k-means.
  2. Designed a heuristic that flags both mislabelled data and model errors.
